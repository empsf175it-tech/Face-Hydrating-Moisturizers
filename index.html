    FAQ

    Good questions, clear answers

    Will these moisturizers feel heavy on oily skin?

    No. Dewdrop Gel Cream and Veil Hyaluron Serum-Cream are water-based and oil-free in feel, built for oily and combination skin.

    Can I use them with retinol or acids?

    Yes. Apply your active first, wait a minute, then seal with Ceramide Calm Lotion or Cloud Barrier Cream to reduce irritation.

    How does auto-reorder work?

    You choose a moisturizer and how often you use it. We calculate when it will run out and schedule the next shipment a few days before. Skip, pause or cancel from your dashboard anytime.

    Are the products suitable for sensitive skin?

    All formulas are fragrance-free and free from common irritants. Ceramide Calm Lotion is designed specifically for reactive skin. Patch-test any new product.

    What is your return policy?

    If your skin doesn't love it, contact us within 30 days for a refund or exchange.
